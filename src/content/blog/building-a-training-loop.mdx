## Reports should not be dead ends

Most analysis tools tell you what went wrong and then stop. A better product turns those mistakes into the next actions.

## The loop

1. Import a game
2. Detect critical moments
3. Cluster mistakes by theme
4. Schedule repeatable drills
5. Measure whether those mistakes happen less often next week

## Why it matters

That loop creates compounding value. It also makes premium features easier to justify because they produce a training system, not just a prettier review screen.
